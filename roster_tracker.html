PASS 1 — MANDATORY Must be dropped at the preseason draft if the player was (1) added to a fantasy roster after the PRIOR season's trade deadline (see NHL section above) AND (2) their ELC-level contract was also signed after that same deadline — a mid-season stash still has to go, regardless of production or cap efficiency. Always drops first, unconditionally. Not tunable here. 25-26/26-27's lists are auto-detected from PP Signings + that season's Xactions (see _computeElcMandatoryDrops) — verified live to exactly reproduce 25-26's known Ivan Demidov case; 23-24/24-25 still use their own hand-curated historical list (no Xactions data synced for those seasons).
PASS 2 — INJURY/DEMOTION Anyone on that season's known-long-term-unavailable list (injured or sent to a lower league) drops unconditionally, same as Pass 1 — a dead roster spot regardless of how good their last-known production/cap efficiency looks. Not tunable here — same hardcoded per-season list the draft side already excludes free agents with.
PASS 3 — REQUIRED MINIMUM Below this total, players drop automatically (worst value first) regardless of value, cap, or demand.

PASS 4 — STOP CONDITIONS Once the floor above and position quotas are clear, these decide when to stop making further optional cuts. Not a drop reason on its own — it ends the process.

PASS 5 — CAP CRUNCH Even past the floor, a player still gets forced out if keeping them would blow the cap. No dedicated setting here — driven by that season's Cap Limit (a different settings section) plus the built-in draft reserve.
PASS 6 — BELOW CURVE VALUE The normal optional cut once the floor's met and cap's fine. A player is protected as a last-resort drop if EITHER their raw pts clears the Drop value floor below OR their ABP is 0 (no better free agent available at their position/cap) — unless their curve-delta falls below the Last-resort delta floor, which overrides both. All three values below work together to decide who's an easy cut vs. protected from one.
